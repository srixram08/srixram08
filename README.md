# 🧠 Featured Project

## ⚡ Vector — AI Decision Intelligence & Assurance Platform

> **A system that thinks before it reacts.**

Vector is an AI-powered decision intelligence platform designed for cloud-native and Kubernetes environments.

Traditional infrastructure systems often react **after** failures occur.

Vector introduces a proactive lifecycle:

```text
Predict
   ↓
Simulate
   ↓
Assure
   ↓
Act
   ↓
Verify
```

### 🔥 Core Architecture

```text
Infrastructure Telemetry
          ↓
     Risk Detection
          ↓
    AI Risk Prediction
          ↓
 Candidate Action Generator
          ↓
  Digital Twin Simulation
          ↓
 Risk + Confidence Analysis
          ↓
   Policy Validation
          ↓
 Decision Assurance Engine
          ↓
    Automated Action
          ↓
 Verification + Rollback
```

### 🚀 Key Features

* 📡 Real-time infrastructure telemetry
* 🔮 Predictive failure detection
* 🤖 AI-generated remediation strategies
* 🪞 Digital Twin simulation
* 🛡️ Decision assurance
* 📊 Risk & confidence assessment
* 📜 Policy-based validation
* 🧠 Explainable AI reasoning
* 🔄 Automated rollback planning
* ☸️ Kubernetes-focused architecture
* ☁️ Cloud-native infrastructure management

### 🎯 Vision

**Make infrastructure intelligent enough to evaluate a decision before executing it.**
